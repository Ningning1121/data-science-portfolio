## 🚀 Project Showcase

### Project: Pakistan Social Media Usage vs Student Performance
**Research Question:** Among young Pakistani students, is heavy social-media use genuinely harmful to academic performance, and if so, through which channel? 

**Visualization:**
![Social Media Dashboard](https://github.com/Ningning1121/data-science-project/blob/main/social_media_dashboard.png?raw=true)

**Brief Explanation of Findings:** 
The visualization reveals a strong negative association between daily social-media hours and exam scores (r ≈ −0.96). The data suggests the harm operates primarily through "displacement" — heavy users are not inherently weaker students, but their scrolling displaces study time and coincides with lower mental well-being. The positive control panel (bottom-right) confirms that study hours strongly boost scores, validating the measurement.
